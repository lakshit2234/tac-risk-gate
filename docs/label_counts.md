# Risk-event label counts (counts only)

Generated 2026-11-20 (UTC) by `python -m tacgate.phase2.label_counts` from our Binance perp 1m klines (2025-07-15 10:30 to 2026-11-18 16:18 UTC). Read-only; no feature, no model. The horizons and thresholds are PROPOSALS from docs/phase2_plan.md, not decisions.

A decision time is every 5-minute boundary. A decision time is positive for a long when the lowest low in the next H hours is at least x below the close at that time, and for a short when the highest high is at least x above it. Positive decision times closer than H are merged into one episode (counted in the month the episode starts). Positive decision times of one crash overlap heavily, so they are NOT independent samples: the number of episodes is the honest count.
